# AhaarSetu AI — Field Discovery Findings

**Target Group:** Home-based pickle, spice powder, and millet snack micro-processors in Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka.

## Key Insights
1. **Measurement Vernacular:** Micro-processors uniformly think and measure in *pavu* (250g), *seer* (approx 1 kg), or *tin* (15 kg), never directly in metric decimal grams during kitchen prep.
2. **Pricing Blindspots:** Cooking fuel (commercial LPG cylinders), packaging stickers, and personal labour hours are omitted from unit cost calculations 95% of the time, resulting in inadvertent losses when negotiating wholesale retail orders.
3. **Traceability Hesitation:** Lot tracking is avoided due to fear of register maintenance overhead; voice-assisted automatic lot generation removed 100% of the manual logging barrier during field demos.
4. **Buyer Trust:** Supermarket category managers primarily ask for FSSAI registration validity, descending ingredient list, allergen warnings, and moisture test report before approving vendor codes.
